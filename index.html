About me

I'm a developer who enjoys turning ideas into working products. I'm currently building my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python, while creating projects that challenge me to learn something new.

I build web applications, chase down tricky problems, and turn ideas into working products. I like picking projects that push me out of my comfort zone, because that's where the best learning happens.

My toolkit includes web development, coding, Microsoft Office, Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, Canva and Figma. I do my best work as part of a team, sharing the journey and the wins.

Off the keyboard, I've never been one to sit still. I represented the Eastern Cape on the provincial basketball team in 2010, lent my voice to a Nedbank recording in 2016, and earned a medal at the 15k marathon hosted by Hot 91.9 in 2018. Whether it's a new language, a new course or a new stage, I'm always ready for the next challenge.
