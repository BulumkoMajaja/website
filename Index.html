About Me

Hello, I'm Bulumko! I'm a software developer who treats every project like a new trail to explore.

I build web applications, chase down tricky problems, and turn ideas into working products. I'm currently sharpening my skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python, and I pick projects that push me out of my comfort zone, because that's where the best learning happens.

My toolkit includes web development, coding and Microsoft Office, and I do my best work as part of a team, sharing the journey and the wins.

Off the keyboard, I've never been one to sit still. I represented the Eastern Cape on the provincial basketball team in 2010, lent my voice to a Nedbank recording in 2016, and earned a medal at the 15k marathon hosted by Hot 91.9 in 2018. That same year, I stepped up to audition at Mix FM 93.8. Whether it's a new language, a new course or a new stage, I'm always ready for the next challenge.
